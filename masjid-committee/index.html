Masjid Committee

مسجد کمیٹی

An app for masjid committees in India to run the masjid's accounts and keep members informed, in English and Urdu.

Members and chandaHouseholds, monthly chanda, receipts and dues in one list.
Prayer timesDaily adhan and jama'at times worked out for the masjid's location.
NoticesAnnouncements and janaza alerts sent to members and followers.
Member loginMembers log in with their phone number and a code sent on WhatsApp.
AccountsIncome, expenses and monthly balance, shared with members if the committee chooses.
Masjids near youFind nearby masjids and follow their notices.

The app is in testing on Google Play. Committees that want to try it can write to hello@sbnapps.com.
