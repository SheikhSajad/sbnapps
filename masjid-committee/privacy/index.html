Masjid Committee privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Masjid Committee app ("the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is made by SBNApps, Kashmir, India ("we"). If you have questions, write to hello@sbnapps.com.

Who uses the app

  • Committee members (for example the chairman or treasurer) manage a masjid's records. They sign in with an email address and password.
  • Masjid members are households the committee has added. They sign in with their mobile number.
  • Followers follow one or more masjids to see notices and prayer times. They can use the app without creating an account.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address (committee members) or mobile number (members and followers who sign in).
  • Records the committee enters: member names, mobile numbers, area, family members, monthly chanda amount, payments and receipts, and the masjid's income and expenses.
  • Things you send in the app: "I have paid" reports and requests to correct your details.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device location is used to work out prayer times and to show masjids near you. We do not keep a history of where you have been. The location of a masjid is saved when a committee adds it.
  • App settings: language, prayer-time method and similar choices, kept on your device.

We do not collect contacts, photos, files, or payment card details. UPI payments open in your own UPI app; the app does not see or store your bank or UPI details.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show members their own chanda, receipts and masjid notices.
  • To let the committee keep its masjid's records.
  • To calculate prayer times and show nearby masjids.

We do not sell your information, and the app has no advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Your masjid's committee can see the member records they keep for that masjid.
  • Members see only their own records. If the committee turns on shared accounts, members see monthly totals only, never another person's payments.
  • Service providers that run the app for us:
    • Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in.
    • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers login codes to your mobile number on WhatsApp.
    • Map tile providers, which supply the map images on the nearby-masjids screen.
    They process data only to provide these services.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules make sure each person can only read the records they are allowed to see.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active or while your masjid's committee keeps your member record. When you delete your account, your sign-in details are deleted. Records a committee keeps about its masjid (for example past chanda receipts) stay with that masjid unless the committee removes them.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Members can ask the committee to correct their details from the Profile screen.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or as explained on the delete account page.

Children

The app is meant for adults who manage or belong to a masjid. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
