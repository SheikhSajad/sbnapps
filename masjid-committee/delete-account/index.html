Account deletion

Delete your Masjid Committee account

You can delete your Masjid Committee account (made by SBNApps) at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Masjid Committee app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (members) or Settings (committee).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Or ask us by email

Email hello@sbnapps.com from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you sign in with. Use the subject "Delete my Masjid Committee account". We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account: email address or mobile number, and your login.
  • Your app settings, and the masjids you follow.
  • For a committee account: the masjid you manage and all of its records (members, chanda, donations, expenses and notices).

What may be kept

  • If you are a member, your masjid's committee may keep its own record of you and your past chanda receipts for the masjid's accounts. Ask your committee if you want that record removed.
  • Information we must keep by law, for no longer than required.
