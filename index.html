Questions people ask us.

Something else on your mind? Send it through the form below and we'll answer it personally.

Do your apps work on both Android and iPhone?

Yes. We build in Flutter, so one codebase ships to Android and iOS with the same screens and speed. Owner dashboards can also run in the browser.

Can the app be in Urdu?

Yes. Masjid Committee is fully bilingual in English and Urdu, with proper Nastaliq lettering and a Hijri calendar. We can do the same for your app.

How do the WhatsApp messages work?

We use the official WhatsApp Cloud API. n8n workflows watch your data and send fee reminders, receipts, order updates and alerts from your business number, automatically.

Is my data kept separate from other customers?

Each organisation is its own tenant. Row-level security in Postgres means a masjid, boutique or library can only ever read and write its own records.

Can you build something that isn't on your product list?

Yes. Most of our products started as one business's problem. Tell us how the work happens today and we'll show you what an app for it would look like.

Where are you based?

Baramulla, Jammu and Kashmir. We work with clients across India, in person around Baramulla and Srinagar and online everywhere else.

Tell us how the work happens today.

A register, a WhatsApp group, a spreadsheet nobody trusts. Describe it in a few lines and we'll come back with how an app could take it over.
